About the Mandir

Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir

The birthplace of Bhagwan Shri Ram, rebuilt in the traditional Nagara style of temple architecture.

ॐ
2024 Pran Pratishtha

A temple 500 years in the making

Built in the Nagara style of North Indian temple architecture, the mandir rises across three floors on a raised plinth. Its 392 pillars and 44 doors are carved from Bansi Paharpur pink sandstone, and notably the structure uses no iron anywhere in its construction.

  • Nagara architecture — 380 ft long, 250 ft wide and 161 ft tall, with five mandapas.
  • Surya Tilak — on Ram Navami, sunlight is engineered to fall on Ram Lalla's forehead at noon.
  • Ram Lalla — the 51-inch idol was consecrated on 22 January 2024.
  • Accessible — ramps and lifts are provided for elderly and differently-abled devotees.

Before You Visit

Rules & Guidelines

Please follow these guidelines to help maintain the sanctity of the temple and keep queues moving safely.

Permitted

  • Valid photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, DL)
  • Prasad purchased at the complex
  • Small purse or wallet
  • Prescription medicines
  • Wheelchairs (available free at gate)

Not Allowed

  • Mobile phones & cameras inside
  • Bags, belts and leather items
  • Outside food, tobacco or alcohol
  • Weapons of any kind
  • Pets and inflammable objects

Dress Code

Traditional and modest clothing is expected. Shoulders and knees should be covered. Footwear must be deposited at the free counters near the entrance.

Locker Facility

Free lockers for phones, bags and footwear are available at all four entry gates. Keep your token safe — it is required to collect your belongings.

Conduct

Maintain silence in the garbhagriha. Do not push in the queue, and please follow the instructions of security personnel and volunteers at all times.

With Children

Keep children with you at all times. Write your mobile number on a band for small children — lost child help desks are located at every gate.

Visitor Help Desk

Our volunteers assist thousands of devotees every day with directions, accessibility and lost belongings.

  • Accessibility support Free wheelchairs, ramps and priority queues for senior citizens and differently-abled devotees.
  • Lost & found Report lost items or persons at any gate help desk, or call the temple helpline.
  • Free facilities Drinking water, restrooms, shoe stands and lockers are provided free of charge.
  • Language assistance Volunteers speak Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali and Marathi.
